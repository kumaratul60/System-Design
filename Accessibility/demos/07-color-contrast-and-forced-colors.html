07: Color Contrast & Forced Colors Mode

Validate WCAG 2.1 Level AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI) vs failing contrast, and simulate Windows High Contrast / Forced Colors Mode and Color Vision Deficiencies (CVD) in real time.

OS / Vision Simulation:

1. Compliant Contrast (Level AA)

High Luminance Ratio PASS: 13.8:1

Light text (#f8fafc) on dark slate background (#0f172a). Easily legible for users with low vision or screen glare.

2. Non-Compliant Contrast (Anti-Pattern)

Low Contrast Copy FAIL: 2.1:1

Low contrast gray-on-dark copy (#64748b on #334155). Unreadable under direct sunlight or for low vision users.
