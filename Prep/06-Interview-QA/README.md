## 🚦 How to Use This Q&A Bank

1. **Active Self-Testing:** Click on any question to expand the answer _only after_ attempting to state your reasoning aloud.
2. **Follow-Up Drills:** Answer the "Follow-Up Drill" questions at the bottom of each answer to simulate interviewer probes.
3. **Trade-off Defense:** Use `Principal-Level-QA.md` and `Tradeoffs-Cheatsheet.md` to practice framing answers as _"I chose X over Y because..."_.
